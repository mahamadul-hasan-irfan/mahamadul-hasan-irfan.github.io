Keeping Water in the Loop

A circular water system for a water-secure world, with practical lessons from Singapore and Bangladesh.

The idea

Most of us use water once and forget it. A circular system treats used water as a resource: collect it, treat it and recover what is valuable, then reuse it for the right purpose, while continuously testing and monitoring for safety.

The loop, step by step

1 · Water Sources2 · Water Use3 · Collect Used Water4 · Treat & Recover5 · Reuse by Purpose6 · Test & Monitor

What I learned

Not every litre needs drinking-water quality. Matching treatment to the intended use, such as industry, cleaning or agriculture, saves cost and protects rivers. Singapore shows that this works at city scale, and Bangladesh can adapt the idea step by step.
